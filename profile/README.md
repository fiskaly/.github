## Hey, this is fiskaly 👋

![fiskaly Logo](https://www.fiskaly.com/fiskaly_logo.svg)

### OUR VISION

> fiskaly – VAT compliance at scale

[fiskaly](https://fiskaly.com) provides cloud-based solutions for fiscalization and VAT compliance across Europe. The secure, country-specific services help POS vendors and operators, retailers, and omni-channel operators meet legal requirements with ease. fiskaly is active in Germany, Austria, Spain, Italy, France, and Sweden, with more than 140 employees.

### OUR MISSION

> We strive to become the best cloud based signature provider.

[fiskaly](https://fiskaly.com) is driven by a culture of innovation. Our mission is to simplify all aspects of fiscalization and VAT-Compliance offering full circle effortless compliance solutions on a Pan-European level: secure, scalable, and cloud-native — empowering businesses to stay compliant while helping governments collect taxes.
